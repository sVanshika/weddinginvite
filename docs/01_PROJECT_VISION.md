# PROJECT_VISION.md

# Project Name

**The Invitation**

---

# Vision

This project is not a wedding website.

It is a cinematic digital invitation designed to recreate the emotional experience of receiving, opening, and reading a handcrafted luxury wedding invitation.

Every interaction should feel intentional, elegant, and timeless.

The visitor should never feel like they are browsing a website.

Instead, they should feel like they are physically interacting with a beautifully crafted invitation.

---

# Core Experience

The invitation is the product.

The invitation is the interface.

The invitation is the navigation.

There is no transition from "invitation" to "website."

Instead, the invitation gradually unfolds and transforms into the storytelling experience.

Everything the visitor reads should appear as though it has always been part of the invitation.

---

# User Journey

The visitor experiences five acts.

## Act I — Receive

The visitor discovers a sealed invitation.

Curiosity is created.

---

## Act II — Open

The envelope opens.

The invitation slowly emerges.

The invitation unfolds.

---

## Act III — Read

The couple's story unfolds.

Wedding details appear naturally.

The visitor never leaves the invitation.

---

## Act IV — Celebrate

Photos, events, venue, and memories are presented as carefully designed chapters.

---

## Act V — Respond

The RSVP concludes the experience.

The invitation gently closes the story.

# Experience Principles

## 1. Physical First

Every animation must have a physical explanation.

Paper unfolds.

Wax reflects light.

Cards slide.

Bookmarks move.

Nothing should animate without purpose.

---

## 2. Continuous Storytelling

The experience should never feel divided into independent web pages.

The visitor should feel as though they are reading a single beautifully designed invitation from beginning to end.

---

## 3. Luxury Through Restraint

Luxury is created through precision, spacing, typography, lighting, and subtle motion.

Avoid excessive effects.

Avoid unnecessary decoration.

Avoid fast animations.

---

## 4. Motion Creates Emotion

Animations should never exist only because scrolling occurred.

Every animation should reinforce the narrative.

Motion should guide attention.

Motion should communicate material.

Motion should create anticipation.

---

## 5. Mobile First

The invitation is expected to be opened primarily through messaging platforms.

The primary experience should be designed for portrait mobile devices.

Tablet and desktop layouts should expand the same experience rather than redesign it.

---

# Technical Goals

The implementation should prioritize:

* Smooth 60 FPS animations
* Responsive layouts
* Accessibility
* Modular React architecture
* Reusable animation timelines
* High Lighthouse scores
* Maintainable code

---

# Visual Goals

The experience should evoke:

* Elegance
* Warmth
* Craftsmanship
* Romance
* Celebration

It should never feel:

* Corporate
* Minimalist
* Generic
* Over-animated
* Template-driven

---

# AI Development Guidelines

Every AI generating code for this project should follow these principles:

* Preserve the illusion of interacting with a real invitation.
* Prefer subtlety over spectacle.
* Never introduce animations that feel playful or bouncy.
* Build reusable components rather than page-specific implementations.
* Separate presentation, animation logic, and content.
* Optimize for readability and maintainability.

---

# Success Criteria

The project is successful if visitors say:

"I forgot I was looking at a website."

Instead, they should feel they experienced a beautifully crafted wedding invitation brought to life through motion and storytelling.
